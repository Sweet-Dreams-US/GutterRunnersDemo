# Design Card, Gutter Runners

## Type

- Display: Big Shoulders Display, weights 800 and 900. Used for the wordmark, headings, and service names.
- Body and interface: Schibsted Grotesk, weights 400, 500, and 700. Used for paragraphs, labels, navigation, forms, and admin controls.
- Both families are self hosted as subset WOFF2 files in `fonts/`.

## Palette

- Rain wash `#F3F5F6`: the light ground and primary page field.
- Storm slate `#26323D`: body text, navigation, hero, work section, and footer.
- Copper `#B8733A`: primary actions, focus rings, links, the formed edge highlight, and the accent rule.
- Aluminum `#C9CED3`: the continuous gutter, cross sections, and light metal surfaces.
- Galvanized `#9AA3AB`: borders, quiet text on slate, and secondary rules.
- Rain blue `#5D8FB8`: water only.

## Archetype

One continuous aluminum run begins as a flat strip, forms into a K style profile, carries the three services on hangers, turns down the page edge, and empties into the estimate request.

## Signature interaction

The forming section morphs one SVG path through five K profile states as the visitor scrolls. It is pinned on wider screens for no more than three view heights. On phones it plays once on entry without pinning. The final formed profile stays visible as the physical line that carries the rest of the page.

## Navigation progress

A thin aluminum K profile runs beneath the sticky navigation. Rain blue water fills the inside from left to right with scroll progress. A few drops fall from its leading edge, then the water reaches a small downspout at the right edge. Reduced motion updates the water length without moving drops.

## Generated assets

1. A 16:9 close view of heavy rain on dark slate roof shingles with no gutter, house, people, or text.
2. A six second rain loop animated from the hero still.
3. A seamless brushed aluminum texture in `#C9CED3`.
4. A seamless copper texture in `#B8733A` with light edge patina.
5. A transparent K style profile technical drawing with dimension ticks and no text.
6. A seamless rain wash ground with faint diagonal rain.
7. A consistent transparent icon set: seamless run, hanger and drill, sealant gun at a joint, and downspout elbow.
8. A transparent soft storm cloud illustration in slate and rain blue.
9. Transparent K profile edge strips for rain wash and storm slate seams.

## Direction check

- Neither type family is on the banned list.
- The layout is one connected run, not a row of cards or a reusable section stack.
- The only progress device is the specified K profile with water.
- There are no numbered eyebrows, drawn page line, scrolling marks, plain progress rectangles, grain overlay, service marquee, or oversized background wordmark.
- Public work photos and comparisons do not render until real images are added in the admin.
